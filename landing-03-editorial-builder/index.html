Approach / actual behaviors

B

Process / four calm moves

Not a timeline for decoration.

The sequence is here to answer what the client receives, what changes, and where the next decision lives.

01

Start with the real brief

02

Test the shape

Early cost, constructability, and material direction while the choices are still kind to change.

03
