A / working premise

Not a slogan. A working habit.

When a project gets complicated, the answer is usually a clearer next decision.

We are designing this prototype around a simple promise: owners should not have to translate a builder’s internal process to know what happens next. Every phase leaves a useful trace—an estimate note, a coordination decision, a mockup, a site update, a handover record.
